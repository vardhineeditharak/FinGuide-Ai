# Cybersecurity Advisory: Prevention of Cyber-Enabled Financial Fraud
*Grounded in the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Direction on Digital Payment Security Controls and Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) Cyber-Safety Advisories.*

---

## 1. Classifications of Active Financial Scams in India

To ensure the safety of your assets, it is critical to recognize the common vectors used by cybercriminals to bypass banking security systems:

### A. Credentials Phishing & Social Engineering (Vishing/Smishing)
* **Modus Operandi:** Fraudsters posing as bank executives, credit card department heads, or regulatory officials contact victims claiming their bank account, card, or wallet is about to be blocked. 
* **Regulatory Fact:** Banking institutions and payment operators are strictly prohibited under RBI guidelines from asking consumers for confidential credentials—including **One-Time Passwords (OTPs)**, **UPI Personal Identification Numbers (UPI PINs)**, or **Card Verification Values (CVVs)**—over the phone, via email, or via SMS.

### B. KYC (Know Your Customer) Expiry Exploitation
* **Modus Operandi:** Threat actors distribute SMS/WhatsApp warnings stating: *"Your bank account has been suspended due to pending KYC update. Click link to complete KYC."* 
* **Security Risk:** Clicking the link redirects the user to a spoofed bank landing page designed to capture net-banking credentials and OTPs.

### C. SIM Swap Fraud
* **Modus Operandi:** Fraudsters obtain duplicate SIM cards of the victim's phone number by submitting forged identity proofs to telecom network providers.
* **Security Risk:** Once the duplicate SIM is active, the victim's actual SIM card loses signal, allowing the fraudster to intercept financial transaction OTPs and drain bank accounts.

### D. Reversal / "Wrong Transfer" QR Code Scams
* **Modus Operandi:** Scammers contact victims claiming they have accidentally transferred money to the victim's account and send a QR code or payment request to "refund" the money.
* **Security Risk:** Scanning a QR code or entering a UPI PIN is exclusively a **debit authorization** (sending money). It is never required to credit (receive) funds.

### E. Unregulated digital lending apps (Instant Loan Fraud)
* **Modus Operandi:** Unofficial, high-interest mobile applications offering instant loans without credit checks. Upon installation, these applications steal contact books, photos, and personal data to blackmail victims during repayment cycles.

## 2. Red Flags and Risk Indicators

Financial users must immediately suspect fraud if any of the following parameters are met:
* **Artificial Urgency:** Demands for immediate action, warnings of immediate legal prosecution, or threat of service cancellation.
* **Request for Credentials:** Any prompt to share CVV, PIN, account passwords, or OTP.
* **Link Discrepancies:** Payment links utilizing URL shorteners (e.g., `bit.ly`, `tinyurl.com`) or misspelled official domains.
* **Asymmetric Caller Information:** Callers who claim credibility by quoting public information (like full name or phone number) but fail to verify internal bank security tokens.

## 3. Mandatory Incident Response Protocol (Immediate Steps)

In the event of a suspected scam or successful unauthorized transaction, users must activate this protocol immediately to minimize loss:

1. **The Golden Hour Rule:** Initiate reporting within the first **one (1) hour** of the fraudulent transaction. Funds intercepted during this window have a significantly higher rate of successful recovery and freezing.
2. **National Cyber Crime Helpline:** Call the official national helpline number **`1930`** immediately. The service is operational 24/7.
3. **Official Reporting Portal:** File a formal incident report online at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: **[cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)**.
4. **Immediate Bank Communication:** Contact your bank's dedicated cyber fraud hotline to freeze all debit operations, block active credit/debit cards, and disable UPI/net-banking channels.
5. **Credential Reset:** Change all passwords, login PINs, and transaction authorization codes immediately from a secure, clean device.

---

## 4. Preventive Financial Hygiene Habits

* **Official App Stores Only:** Only install banking applications from verified platforms (Google Play Store, Apple App Store). Never download or install `.apk` files shared via social platforms or messaging apps.
* **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Set up biometrics, secure patterns, or secondary confirmation prompts for all transaction steps.
* **Regular Credit Monitoring:** Review credit reports monthly via registered credit bureaus to check for unauthorized loan accounts opened in your name.
* **Verify Merchant Authenticity:** Check the domain reputation and business registration of online payment gateways before performing checkout transactions.
